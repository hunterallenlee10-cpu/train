# train. (Train), Ocean City, MD

> Private garage gym and personal training space in Ocean City, Maryland. Two-hour blocks, 8 people max per block, no class and no instructor.

## Passes

- Drop-In: $25 per person. A one-time 2 hour block for you to Train.
- 3-Pack: $69 per person. For the Weekend Warriors in town.
- Train Unlimited: $99 a month. Unlimited access, one flat monthly price. No GTFO fee ever. I mean it.
- Guests: members can add a guest to their block for $25.

## Visit

- Address: 108 142nd St., Unit C, Ocean City, MD 21842. Bayside, in a newly built garage between Coastal Highway and Sinepuxent Ave.
- Blocks: 4:00am to 10:00pm, nine 2-hour blocks a day.
- Parking: Spots right out front, then Sinepuxent Ave.
- Email: info@trainocmd.com
